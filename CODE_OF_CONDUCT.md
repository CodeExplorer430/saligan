# Code of Conduct

## Standard

SALIGAN contributors MUST maintain a respectful, inclusive, technically focused environment.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and disclosure of private or sensitive data.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to maintainers through their verified GitHub profiles:

- `@chesternothacker`;
- `@codeexplorer430`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation. Reports MUST be handled with minimum necessary disclosure.

This policy applies to repository spaces and project representation in public spaces.
