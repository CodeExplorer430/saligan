# 00 — Project Identity and Naming

## Selected project name

**SALIGAN**

## Full name

**Student Attendance, Logbook, Internship Goals, Accomplishments, and Notes**

## Subtitle

**An Offline-First, Self-Hosted Internship Records and OJT Management Platform**

## Meaning

**SALIGAN** is an acronym built from the platform's main concepts:

| Letter | Meaning | Product feature represented |
|---|---|---|
| S | Student | Student-centered intern workflow |
| A | Attendance | Time-in, break, time-out, DTR, undertime, overtime |
| L | Logbook | Daily and weekly internship records |
| I | Internship | OJT plans, required hours, roles, supervisors, companies |
| G | Goals | Target hours, expected completion, progress tracking |
| A | Accomplishments | Daily/weekly outputs, proof images, documentation |
| N | Notes | Work notes, reflections, correction reasons, report drafts |

The word **saligan** also carries the idea of a basis or foundation. That makes the name suitable for a platform that becomes the foundation for trustworthy internship records.

## Positioning statement

SALIGAN is a free and open-source, offline-first internship records and OJT management platform for students who need a reliable way to record attendance, compute rendered and remaining hours, document accomplishments, and produce submission-ready DTR and report exports.

## Repository description

```text
Offline-first, self-hostable internship records and OJT management platform for DTR, rendered hours, reports, and supervisor review.
```

## Brand tone

- Filipino-rooted, but understandable to international open-source users.
- SaaS-like in product quality, but FOSS and self-hostable by default.
- Practical, documentation-first, and student-friendly.
- Built by interns who directly experienced the manual tracking problem.
- Serious enough for schools, companies, and future contributors.

## Names rejected

| Name | Reason rejected |
|---|---|
| OJTala | Catchy, but it looked like a forced word combination. |
| Talaan | Strong Filipino word, but not an English acronym. |
| OJTMS | Clear, but too generic and less brandable. |
| DTRMS | Too narrow because the product is more than DTR. |
| SULO | Good brand idea, but SALIGAN sounds more institutional and foundational. |
| TALA | Good records identity, but SALIGAN covers attendance, logbook, goals, accomplishments, and notes more completely. |

## Final naming decision

Use **SALIGAN** consistently across README, documentation, diagrams, OpenAPI, package metadata, prompts, ADRs, and future code.
